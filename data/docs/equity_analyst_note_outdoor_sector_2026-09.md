## Key takeaways
Sector checks point to softer full-price demand in outdoor apparel heading into Q4, with brands leaning on promotions to clear fall inventory. We expect promotional intensity to remain elevated through the holiday season, which pressures gross margins across the category.

## Wholesale channel
Large retail partners are ordering closer to need and pushing back delivery windows to manage their own inventory. We see an elevated risk of order deferrals and cancellations among mid-sized wholesale brands, particularly for late-arriving seasonal goods.

## Costs
Freight costs are a headwind for the sector in Q4. Our channel checks suggest peak surcharges could persist through December, which is longer than many company guidance assumptions.
