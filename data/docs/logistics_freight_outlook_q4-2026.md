## Market conditions
Domestic parcel and LTL carriers announced peak-season surcharges earlier than usual this year, citing port congestion on the West Coast and tight trucking capacity. Ridgeline's two primary carriers began applying surcharges on September 1.

## Expected cost path
We expect freight cost per unit to stay elevated through October. In addition, our main parcel carrier has announced a 4.5% general rate increase (GRI) effective October 1. Our base case is that surcharges ease in mid-November as import volumes normalize, which would bring freight cost per unit back toward budget by December.

## Mitigations under evaluation
1. Run an RFP for a secondary regional LTL carrier; bids are due October 10 and a decision is targeted for October 20.
2. Shift DTC orders from 2-day to ground shipping where the customer promise allows; estimated savings of 8-10% on DTC parcel spend.
3. Stop expedited air freight unless approved by the COO.

## Risks
If supplier delays continue, we will face another choice between expedited freight cost and missed wholesale ship dates. Upstream, two of our three outerwear suppliers have flagged possible October production delays.
