## Summary
September operating profit landed well below budget. Preliminary close shows the shortfall is concentrated in wholesale revenue timing, freight and fulfillment cost, and marketing spend. Materials cost was favorable to budget, but only because shipped volume was lower; materials cost as a percentage of gross revenue was essentially on plan at about 38%.

## Wholesale revenue
Wholesale revenue missed budget by roughly $190K. About $182K of the miss is a single purchase order from Summit Retail Group (fall outerwear replenishment) that did not ship before month-end. The order was held because the fleece-lined jacket SKUs arrived late from our Vietnam supplier. Sales confirms the customer has not cancelled; the revised ship date is October 14. The remaining gap is small and spread across independent dealers.

## Direct-to-consumer revenue and discounts
DTC gross revenue beat budget by about 5% on the strength of the Fall Trail promotion (20% off sitewide, Sept 12-22). The promotion was deeper than the 10-15% assumed in budget, so discounts and returns were also unfavorable. Net of discounts, the promotion added only a small amount of revenue. The DTC return rate rose to 7.9% vs 5.5% budget; customer service attributes most returns to sizing complaints on the new women's rain shell.

## Freight and fulfillment
Freight and fulfillment cost ran at roughly 9.3% of gross revenue vs 6.0% budget. Two causes: (1) our primary LTL and parcel carriers applied peak-season surcharges starting September 1, about a month earlier than prior years, and (2) we paid for expedited air freight on the late fleece-lined jackets to protect the Summit Retail order. Operations estimates the expedited air shipments alone cost about $21K.

## Operating expenses
Marketing was about $44K over budget because the Fall Trail paid social and influencer campaign originally planned for October was pulled into September. Marketing views this as a timing shift, not incremental spend, and expects October marketing to come in under budget by a similar amount. Salaries and benefits were modestly over budget due to nine temporary warehouse staff hired to handle the promotion volume.

## Open items
FP&A has not yet reviewed the remaining opex lines in detail; commentary on those will follow with the final close.
